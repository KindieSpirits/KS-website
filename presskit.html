Description

"Grab your friends and clock in as adorable Guard Bots in the most extravagant museum. Set up exhibitions and patrol the halls, but beware — when the lights go out, the exhibits wander off and cause expensive mayhem. Can you contain the damage to Mr. Veneer's pockets or will he have to “upgrade” you?"
  • ◈
    Co-op survival horror for 1–4 players

    Clock in solo or squad up with 3 friends. Complete your shift objectives before sunset — every task left unfinished makes the coming night twice as deadly.

  • ◈
    Day shift: Curate & Cleanse

    Follow daily exhibition guidelines, arrange artefacts, and track down infected props before nightfall. Every cursed prop you fail to contain becomes a threat once the lights go out.

  • ◈
    Night shift: Survive & Contain

    Possessed exhibits roam the halls. A boss prop stalks every floor. Infection spreads on contact. Protect valuables and destroy what you can't contain — don't let museum value hit zero.

  • ◈
    Procedurally generated museum floors

    Every shift generates a new floor around the central Security Room. Exhibit rooms, utility rooms, and threats change each run.

  • ◈
    Museum value tracking

    Damage tracked in real time by Mr. Veneer himself. If museum value drops below threshold — game over. Guard Bots are replaceable.

  • ◈
    Customisable Guard Bots

    Body variations, colour palettes, accessories, helmets, emotes. Find cosmetics on the floor or purchase them in the Gift Shop.

History

> Origins

KindieSpirits was born in 2026 from a personal challenge. After leaving his own gaming company in Italy, our founder decided to start over on his own terms — to prove he could build something right, his way. What started as a one-man mission grew into something much more serious when his sister joined to handle the art, turning a solo struggle into a family journey. That's where the name comes from: Kin (family) and Indie.

> How Veneer V∅lt happened

Veneer V∅lt started as a prototype inspired by REPO and Observation Duty. The core idea — adorable robots failing to prevent expensive disasters — refused to go away. It went through countless iterations. Mr. Veneer got involved. Things escalated. The studio is fully independent, self-funded, and unapologetically self-published, built to prove that starting over is always possible, even when everything seems lost.

> Current status

Veneer V∅lt is actively in development. The Steam page is live and wishlisting is open. The team is based in Germany — italians with good internet and decent amounts of cats.
