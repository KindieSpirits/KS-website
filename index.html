Lights out. Exhibits possessed. Clock is ticking.

When darkness shrouds the museum halls, unknown forces awaken and wreak havoc. Don't panic — although you weren't trained for this, the Security Room and Depo are equipped with everything you need to survive the night. Infected props transform into hostile entities, intelligent bosses stalk every floor, and infection spreads on contact.

// Known exhibit behaviours:
  • Infected props transforming into hostile entities
  • A boss prop stalking the halls every night
  • Infection spreading on contact between exhibits
  • Mr. Veneer monitoring museum value in real time
  • Upgrade threshold: classified

Coordinate with your crew, prioritise containment, and whatever you do — don't let museum value hit zero.
