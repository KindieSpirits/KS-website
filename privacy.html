Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your data.

Last updated: August 20, 2026

1. Who we are 2. What data we collect 3. Why we process it (legal basis) 4. Cookies & third-party embeds 5. Who we share data with 6. How long we keep it 7. International transfers 8. Your rights 9. Children's privacy 10. Changes to this policy 11. Contact

> 1. Who we are

This website (kindiespirits.com) is operated by KindieSpirits, an independent game studio based in Germany. For questions about this policy or your data, see the Contact section below. Full legal identification details are available in our Impressum.

KindieSpirits is currently transitioning to a registered freelance business (Kleingewerbe/Kleinunternehmer) in Germany. Until registration is complete, Marco Giannitti acts as the data controller in a personal capacity for the purposes of this website.

> 2. What data we collect

Contact form

When you use the contact form on this site, we collect the information you submit: your name, email address, subject, and message. This is processed via Netlify Forms, our website hosting provider's built-in form-handling service. A hidden honeypot field is used only to filter spam submissions and does not collect personal data about you.

Automatic technical data

Like most websites, our hosting provider (Netlify) automatically logs basic technical information for security and operational purposes — such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps. We do not access or use these logs for marketing or profiling.

No analytics or advertising trackers

We do not use Google Analytics, advertising pixels, or any similar tracking or profiling tools on this website.

> 3. Why we process it (legal basis)

We process contact form data under Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps necessary to respond to your request) and, where applicable, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in handling enquiries and preventing spam/abuse). Technical hosting logs are processed under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in keeping the site secure and functional).

> 4. Cookies & third-party embeds

This website does not set cookies or use local storage for analytics, advertising, or tracking purposes. The contact form does not require cookies to function.

Our Press Kit page includes an optional embedded YouTube video. To protect your privacy, the video is not loaded automatically. It is only loaded — and only then may set cookies from YouTube/Google (via the youtube-nocookie.com privacy-enhanced domain) — if you actively click the "load video" button. By clicking, you agree to YouTube's own terms and privacy practices; see Google's Privacy Policy for details.

> 5. Who we share data with

We use the following processors to run this website:

  • Netlify, Inc. — website hosting and contact form processing.
  • Google LLC (YouTube) — only if you choose to load the video embed on the Press Kit page.

We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

> 6. How long we keep it

Contact form submissions are kept only as long as needed to handle your enquiry and for a reasonable follow-up period, after which they are deleted. You can request earlier deletion at any time (see Your rights).

> 7. International transfers

Netlify and Google are based in the United States. Where data is transferred outside the EU/EEA, these providers rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses and/or participation in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

> 8. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Restrict or object to our processing
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

> 9. Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them through the contact form.

> 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add new features or tools to the site. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest revision.
